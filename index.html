    I am a post-doctoral researcher with School of Future Technology (未来技术学院) at South China University of Technology (华南理工大学), working with Prof. Mingkui Tan (谭明奎) and Prof. Cen Chen (陈岑). I received my bachelor and PHD degree from South China University of Technology in 2018 and 2024, respectively. I published papers in top venues, including ICLR, ICML, CVPR, AAAI, IEEE TPAMI, IEEE TCSVT and Neural Networks. As of now, my research has been cited more than 2000+ times, as reported by Google Scholar. I served as the reviewer for several top-tier machine learning/computer vision conferences, including ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, AAAI and etc. My research interests are broadly in machine learning, with a particular focus on the design, optimization, and generalization of structures and architectures across physical and learning systems, including:

    • Deep Model Architecture and Design (for Large Language Models and Foundation Models)
    • Model Generalization, Adaptation (for Robustness under Data Distribution Shifts)
    • Intelligent Electromagnetic Structure Design and Optimization (for Filtering Antennas and RF/Microwave Circuits and Chips)

    I am open to research collaborations and co-supervising students on topics related to intelligent electromagnetic structure design/search, efficient neural architecture design and optimization, and machine learning.
